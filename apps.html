Finger Paint Java

A drawing app with a tree-based undo/redo system. Branch your canvas history and navigate between alternate timelines of your painting.

  • Freehand drawing with adjustable brush sizes and 12-color palette
  • Multi-branch undo/redo with tree visualization
  • Multi-touch pan and zoom
  • Save and load paintings as JSON

Allergen Buddy Kotlin Compose

Scan product barcodes or ingredient labels with your camera to check for allergens. Matches against a configurable list of personal sensitivities.

  • Barcode scanning and OCR text recognition
  • Open Food Facts API integration
  • Configurable allergen/sensitivity profiles
  • Backup and restore sensitivity data
