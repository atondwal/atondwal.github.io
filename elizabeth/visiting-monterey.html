Visiting Monterey County

Carmel-by-the-Sea sits at the northernmost tip of California's Big Sur. While in Monterey County, we highly recommend driving down Highway 1 and taking in the world-famous coastline. Some recommended stops:

4 miles from Carmel: Point Lobos State Natural Reserve

9 miles from Carmel: Garrapatta State Park Bluffs Trail

15 miles from Carmel: Bixby Bridge

26 miles from Carmel: Town of Big Sur (general store, gas, restaurants)

30 miles from Carmel: Nepenthe Restaurant

31 miles from Carmel: Henry Miller Memorial Library

37 miles from Carmel: Julia Pfeiffer Burns State Park, home to McWay Falls
